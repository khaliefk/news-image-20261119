# Thursday 19 November 2026 — the world in one image

A newspaper-style front page summing up Thursday 19 November 2026 in six
stories, drawn as illustrated panels at 1024x1024. `main.ts` (Node 22,
standard library only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`; it uses
no network and no randomness, so two runs write identical bytes. The image
was generated by the agent with an image model from the day's verified
reporting; the prompt and both attempts are in `generate.md`.

Layout: a full-width lead story (the first-ever Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral)
above five stories in a 3 + 2 grid, each with its own illustration and
headline. All six were verified against the sources below.

## The six stories

1. **Three leaders, one table** — US President Donald Trump, China's
   President Xi Jinping and Russia's President Vladimir Putin meet in
   Shenzhen on Thursday 19 November for the first trilateral summit of
   the three leaders, on the sidelines of the APEC summit in China.
   Nuclear arms control and the Iran war are on the agenda; Ukraine is
   not. The White House says no agreement is expected.
   Sources: Reuters, "Trump, Xi, Putin to hold first-ever trilateral
   summit in Shenzhen" (18 Nov 2026); Le Monde (18 Nov 2026); The Hindu
   Business Line (16 Nov 2026).
2. **GTA VI is here** — Grand Theft Auto VI begins its global rollout on
   Thursday 19 November, launching at midnight in each time zone starting
   with New Zealand, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The standard
   edition costs $79.99. Pre-orders are reported at about $1.5 billion,
   and the game holds a 99/100 Metacritic score.
   Sources: Associated Press (19 Nov 2026); Reuters; Polygon.
3. **Anthropic debuts at $83** — AI company Anthropic starts trading on
   the Nasdaq on Thursday 19 November under the ticker ANT. Shares were
   priced at $83, above the $70-78 range, in an offering of 980 million
   shares.
   Sources: Anthropic press release (18 Nov 2026); CNBC; Bloomberg.
4. **Europe rallies in Berlin** — Leaders of the "Coalition of the
   Willing" meet in Berlin on Thursday 19 November: Germany, France,
   Britain, Poland and Italy, plus NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte and
   US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner. The meeting follows the
   European Commission's proposal of a 45 billion euro loan for Ukraine.
   Sources: Reuters (19 Nov 2026, 00:11 UTC).
5. **Cyclone Akvan nears India** — Severe cyclonic storm Akvan moves
   toward India's east coast on Thursday 19 November, with winds of
   90-100 km/h gusting to 110 km/h. Red alerts are in force in six
   Andhra Pradesh districts, with extremely heavy rain forecast for
   19-20 November and landfall expected around noon on 20 November
   between Narsapur and Puri.
   Sources: India Meteorological Department bulletin (19 Nov 2026,
   02:30); The Hindu.
6. **India-Japan talk defence** — India and Japan hold their fourth
   foreign and defence ministers' 2+2 dialogue in Tokyo on Thursday
   19 November. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and External Affairs
   Minister S. Jaishankar meet Japan's Defence Minister Shinjiro
   Koizumi and Foreign Minister Toshimitsu Motegi to review defence
   cooperation and Indo-Pacific security.
   Sources: The Hindu BusinessLine (18 Nov 2026); Press Information
   Bureau, Government of India.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`out/entry.png` is the 1024x1024 JPEG (about 283 KiB, under the 1 MiB cap).
